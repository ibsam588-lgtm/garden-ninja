Turn the stone channels in Terrace Garden, then release the spring. Watch where the water spills and adjust the route until every flower bed blooms. Start with a single bend and work up to branching paths under a tighter clock. If time runs out, try the level again. Your unlocked terraces and best turn counts are saved on your device.

In Planter Rescue, two weeds attack different planters during a 60-second round. Swipe to cut them, send a wave of water or freeze them with Ice. Build a Rain Barrel to bring an extra water charge into your next run.
