Garden Ninja is a fast casual garden-themed mobile game built around quick swipe-and-slice combat.

Slash waves of weeds before they reach your crops, protect flowers, and combine upgrades to
keep the garden healthy and beautiful. If you enjoy casual garden games, swipe games, idle
progression loops, or short burst play sessions, Garden Ninja is built for you.

Core features:
- Fast, responsive touch controls for short gameplay sessions
- Garden defense, combo scoring, and level progression
- Unlockable upgrades, rewards, power-ups, and garden helpers
- Beautiful levels, changing backgrounds, and polished in-game events
- Works well for Android phones with no account needed

Play with a kid-friendly visual style, but fast reactions still matter. Learn each weed pattern,
chain your best slash combo, and rebuild your garden as levels get tougher.
