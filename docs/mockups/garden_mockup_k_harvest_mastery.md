# Garden Ninja — plan the garden, master the harvest

Design mockup for review. This concept replaces the child-oriented visitor activity with a garden that combines planning, execution and permanent development.

## The three screens

1. **Plan your garden.** Arrange crops and choose an order. The pictured order requests 12 strawberries for 180 coins. The wallet begins at 840. Plant and Build are secondary actions; Start harvest is the primary action.
2. **Chain the harvest.** A deliberately started 60-second challenge uses one continuous swipe to connect matching ripe crops. The pictured gesture links five strawberries while the order remains at 7/12 until release. Releasing completes the order. Unripe fruit interrupts the chain.
3. **Build your next advantage.** Completing the order increases the wallet to 1,020. Choose a 900-coin greenhouse to unlock rare crops or a 900-coin terrace to add two planting beds. The selected upgrade is previewed in its actual garden location.

## Where the depth comes from

- **Planning:** adjacent matching crops allow longer routes; mixed planting provides more order options.
- **Execution:** route the swipe through ripe targets without crossing green fruit. A longer clean gesture gathers more crops in less time.
- **Decisions:** choose between crop variety and growing space. Both upgrades permanently change the garden and future runs.
- **Mastery:** improve route efficiency and completed orders per round as crop arrangements and ripeness patterns vary.

## Proposed interaction rules

Touch a ripe target, drag through matching ripe targets, release to gather. Each target can count once per gesture. Selected targets get an outline, a distinct ripe-fruit appearance and a connecting trail. Ripe and unripe fruit differ in shape/size and marking as well as color.

An invalid target ends the current valid chain without removing previously earned order progress. No plant is destroyed. Tap-to-collect is an alternative for players who find tracing difficult; its target rules are the same.

The order counter updates on release. Completing an order automatically awards its coins. Within an active challenge, another order can follow without opening a shop or inventory.

The timer applies only to a challenge the player starts. Planting, arranging and building are untimed. A round ends cleanly when its time expires; completed earnings remain.

## Persistence and replay

Beds, crop selections, layout and built upgrades persist. Challenge crop availability resets for another run, so play does not require hours of waiting or consumable energy. Harvesting leaves the plants rooted. This makes a player-designed garden the basis for repeatable harvest challenges.

The reasons to return are further development of a personally arranged garden, unlocking different crops, and improving performance. These are design hypotheses, not measured retention results.

## Simplicity boundaries

One active order, one spendable currency, direct gestures and contextual planting/build choices. No watering/sun/harvest tool switching during the round. Additional crop complexity appears gradually through upgrades.

The prices, round duration and reward amounts are illustrative design values for playtesting.

## Art direction and files

More mature stylized isometric garden with natural plants, stone, timber, copper and restrained effects. Compact modern typography, legible targets and an unobtrusive HUD.
